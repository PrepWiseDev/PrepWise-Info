# Stories — real anecdotes for PrepWise content

> **Status: STUBS ONLY. ARCHITECT wrote the prompts; Trent writes the stories.**
> Created 2026-07-26.
>
> **Nothing in this file may be published until it is filled in by Trent.**
> Every slot below is empty on purpose. A story is the one thing in the content
> pipeline that cannot be derived, inferred, or generated: it either happened or
> it did not. Inventing one is the single worst thing a writer can do to this
> brand, because it is the part a reader remembers and repeats.

---

## Rules

1. **Never invent a story.** Not a composite, not a "typical user", not a
   plausible scenario written in past tense. If no story in this file fits the
   page, write the page without one.
2. **One story per page, maximum.**
3. **Every story needs one concrete, checkable detail** — a day, a specific
   food, a price, a real moment. Detail is what separates a story from an
   anecdote-shaped paragraph.
4. **Adapt, do not recite.** Use the bones and fit them to the page.
5. **No third-party names without permission.** Friends, testers, and early
   users are "a friend", "an early tester", unless they have said yes.
6. Stories are about the kitchen and the building of the app. Not about health
   outcomes (see the hard claim ban in [`voice.md`](./voice.md)).
